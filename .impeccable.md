## Design Context

### Users
Anime watchers, from casual fans to dedicated otakus, who want a fast, modern, and visually appealing catalog to browse, discover, and track anime.

### Brand Personality
Modern, premium, cinematic.

### Aesthetic Direction
Clean and minimal UI that lets the colorful anime artwork shine. Strong contrast, bold typography for headings, and a focus on dark mode for a cinematic feel (similar to Netflix or modern streaming apps). Avoid overly cluttered layouts.

### Design Principles
1. **Content First:** Let the vibrant anime posters and art be the focus; the UI should frame it, not compete with it.
2. **Cinematic Feel:** Use a polished, immersive dark mode as a primary experience.
3. **Snappy & Responsive:** Interactions should feel instant and fluid, avoiding unnecessary loading states.
4. **Clean Typography:** Use bold, legible typography that establishes clear hierarchy.
